## Overview

This webinar will cover two major pieces of work conducted in 2025 by the Advanced Research Computing team at the University of Birmingham in 2025, to their private Ethernet network. The first objective was to transition from a dependency on a single-site to having cross-site redundancy, and the second was to implement a converged Ethernet network specifically to support Remote Direct Memory Access (RDMA) applications. We will discuss the implementation of these changes, the impact they had, and the lessons learnt during this work.

During the webinar there will be an opportunity to ask questions.

## Who is the webinar for?

Anyone working in research computing infrastructure.

## Speaker

Salih MSA is a Senior Research Infrastructure Engineer in the Advanced Research Computing team at the University of Birmingham, having joined the University in 2023 as a Graduate Systems Engineer. Salih's primary responsibilities are the design, development, and operation of their cloud and networking infrastructure. Additionally, Salih contributes to the maintenance of their high-performance computing platform and the development of wider infrastructure provisioning and automation systems.
