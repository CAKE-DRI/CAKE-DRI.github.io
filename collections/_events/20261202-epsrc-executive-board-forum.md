## Event information

<strong> *EPSRC Update: A community forum with the Executive Board on Wednesday 2nd of December, 3pm-4:30pm* </strong>

EPSRC's Executive Board will be hosting a webinar for the UK engineering and physical sciences community. As well as providing an update and discussing priorities going forward, the Executive Board will be answering your questions as part of a Q&A session.

Questions will be taken during the event, but EPSRC would also welcome them in advance via the registration form.
