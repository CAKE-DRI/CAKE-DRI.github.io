## Event information

<strong> *WP1 Performance Assessment - Open Meeting on 24th of September, 3pm-4pm* </strong> 

At this Work Package 1 (Performance Assessment) Open Meeting, we will discuss and, where appropriate, approve the new tasks proposed for the work package. We will also use the meeting to gather ideas for additional activities, discuss priorities, and agree on the direction of travel for WP1. 

If you would like to <strong>[suggest a new task for discussion](https://shareing-dri.github.io/about/suggest-a-task), please submit it by 22 September</strong>. This will give us time to collate the suggestions and prepare them for discussion during the meeting.
