3:00-4:00pm, 15th October 2026
Online

Overview

Recently completed NFCS+ projects investigated future federation of data transfer and sharing. Join this webinar to find out about the projects and their outcomes and learn about insights into good practice for enabling and facilitating large scale user-driven data transfer and sharing.

During the webinar there will be an opportunity to ask questions.

Who is the webinar for?

Anyone working in research computing infrastructure who is interested in learning more data transfer and sharing.

Speaker

Prof. Basden is Director of the COSMA HPC system at Durham University, which is part of the DiRAC facility, for which he chairs the Technical Directorate.
