**What is CIUK?**

Computing Insight UK (CIUK), formerly known as the Machine Evaluation Workshop, has been running since 1990. It combines an exhibition of the latest High Performance Computing (HPC) hardware and software with a programme of presentations from users describing how HPC and associated technologies have helped them tackle real-world problems.

CIUK 2026 takes place at **Manchester Central Convention Complex from Wednesday 2 to Friday 4 December 2026**, under the theme **Grand Challenges for the Next Decade: Software, People, Research and Economic Growth**. The [full programme](https://www.sc.stfc.ac.uk/ciuk-2026-programme/) is now available ([PDF](https://www.sc.stfc.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/CIUK2026_Programme.pdf)).

## Day Zero: Wednesday 2 December

### Practical Sustainability for Research Computing Facilities: An Action Plan Workshop

**When:** Wednesday 2 December, 13:00 - 15:00

**Where:** CIUK Breakout Room, Exchange Rooms 1 & 2 (upstairs), Manchester Central

**Organiser:** Alex Owen (Queen Mary University of London), with the [NetDRIVE](https://durham.readthedocs.io/en/latest/netdrive/index.html) champions

A hands-on workshop for people who run research computing facilities, focused on turning sustainability ambitions into a practical action plan for your own facility.

### Technical / SysAdmin Meet Up 2026

**When:** Wednesday 2 December, 13:00 - 17:00

**Where:** Barbirolli Room, Bridgewater Hall

**Organiser:** Simon Atack, in association with HPC-SIG and the [ACIT Hub](/landscape-skills-careers/acit/)

An informal meet up for system administrators and technical staff who build and run research computing services. It is open to all, including exhibitors.

There is no registration for the meetup itself, but if you would like lunch please [register for it here](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/e/xw5ZHqhaCq).

### Generative AI and dRTP Skills - Exploring the Landscape of Shifting Skills

**When:** Wednesday 2 December, 15:30 - 17:30

**Where:** CIUK Breakout Room, Exchange Rooms 1 & 2 (upstairs), Manchester Central

**Organiser:** Weronika Filinger (EPCC, University of Edinburgh), for [CHARTED](/landscape-skills-careers/charted/) and [DRIFT](/landscape-skills-careers/drift/)

Generative AI is changing what digital Research Technical Professionals (dRTPs) do day to day, and the skills they need to do it. This session explores how that landscape is shifting and what it means for training and careers.

## Thursday 3 December

### What can Federation do for me? The NFCS Roadmap

**When:** Thursday 3 December, 11:30 - 13:00

**Where:** CIUK Breakout Room, Exchange Rooms 8 - 10 (upstairs), Manchester Central

**Organiser:** Kieran Leach (EPCC, University of Edinburgh), for the [National Federated Compute Services NetworkPlus](/landscape-infrastructure/nfcs/)

The NFCS NetworkPlus has been co-developing a roadmap towards a national federation of compute services. Come and find out what federation could mean for you, whether you use, run or fund research computing.

### A Network + Update Session

**When:** Thursday 3 December, 14:00 - 15:30

**Where:** Main programme, Session 3

A chance to hear what the UKRI DRI programme and its NetworkPlus projects have been up to, and how you can get involved.

| Speaker | Talk |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| Nick Brown (EPCC, CAKE) | The UK Digital Research Infrastructure programme: progress, highlights and how to get involved |

### What is the future of Fortran-based science and engineering?

**When:** Thursday 3 December, 14:00 - 15:30 (running in parallel with the Network + Update Session)

**Where:** CIUK Breakout Room, Exchange Rooms 8 - 10 (upstairs), Manchester Central

**Organiser:** Austen Rainer (Queen's University Belfast), for the [FortranFuture Network+](/landscape-skills-careers/fortran_futures/)

Fortran still underpins a great deal of UK science and engineering. This breakout looks at how we sustain Fortran-dependent research so that it keeps advancing for decades to come. If you're in Manchester early, don't miss the [70 years of Fortran celebration](/events/202612-FortranBirthday/) on Tuesday 1 December.
