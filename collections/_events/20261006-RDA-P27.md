The **Research Data Alliance 27th Plenary Meeting (P27)** takes place **6–8 October 2026** at the **Kia Oval, London**. This year's plenary is hosted by **UK Research and Innovation (UKRI)** and is an in-person event, the first in a new pattern for RDA that pairs an in-person plenary in the autumn with a fully virtual one in the spring.

Under the theme **Shaping a global open research commons**, P27 brings together the international RDA community to explore how to collectively build a strong, connected and sustainable open research ecosystem. Digital research infrastructure sits at the heart of this: linking researchers and innovators to the data, tools, computing resources and skills that drive discovery.

**CAKE is exhibiting at P27.** Come and visit our stand to find out about the UKRI Digital Research Infrastructure (DRI) Programme and how CAKE helps connect it — including the [DRI Landscape](/landscape/), shared [resources and templates in CAKEbox](/resources/), our [knowledge-exchange fellowships](/about/ke-fellowships/) and [placements and visits](/about/placements/), and upcoming [events](/events/) and [funding opportunities](/opportunities/) across the community. We're keen to meet UK DRI projects and international colleagues alike.

Other confirmed exhibitors and sponsors include the Physical Sciences Data Infrastructure (PSDI), the Digital Curation Centre (DCC), RDA-US, Crossref and the Dutch Research Council (NWO).

**Programme highlights:**

- Three days of RDA Working Group, Interest Group and Birds of a Feather sessions, plus new Ideas Pitch sessions
- A poster exhibition featuring over 100 posters from more than 30 countries
- Co-located events on 5 and 9 October, including sessions on AI in research, long-term data preservation, and ethical data and the global research commons

**Audience:** Researchers, data professionals, infrastructure providers, librarians, funders, policy makers and anyone working to make research data easier to share and reuse.

**Format:** In person only

**Location:** Kia Oval, London, UK
