## Event information

<strong> *WP2 Technical Training - Open Meeting on 17th of December, 3pm-4pm* </strong> 

At this Work Package 2 (Technical Training) Open Meeting, we will discuss and, where appropriate, approve the new tasks proposed for the work package. We will also use the meeting to gather ideas for additional activities, discuss priorities, and agree on the direction of travel for WP2.

The aim is to make sure that WP2 reflects the needs of the wider dRTP community and supports the development of technical skills, learning pathways and opportunities for progression across different roles and levels of experience.
