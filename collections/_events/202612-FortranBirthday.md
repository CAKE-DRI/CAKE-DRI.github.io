Fortran has been supporting scientific software engineering and critical
infrastructure for 70 years. To celebrate this, the
[Institute of Computing for Climate Science (ICCS)](https://iccs.cam.ac.uk/)
will be holding a 'birthday party' on 1 December 2026 at the
[Science and Industry Museum](https://www.scienceandindustrymuseum.org.uk/)
in Manchester, UK. The timing was chosen to align with
[Computing Insights UK (CIUK)](https://www.sc.stfc.ac.uk/ciuk-2026/) - 'the UK's
premier conference for HPC and associated research' - which will be held a short
walk away between 2-4 December 2026.

For all details, see the
[event webpage](https://fortran-index.github.io/fortran-index/page/fortran_birthday.html).

Attendance will be free of charge, thanks to the support of the event sponsors:
* [AMD](https://www.amd.com/en.html) (platinum sponsor)
* [Dell](https://www.dell.com/en-uk) (platinum sponsor)
* [CAKE](https://www.cake.ac.uk/) (bronze sponsor)
* [OpenMP](https://www.openmp.org/) (bronze sponsor)
* [ICCS](https://iccs.cam.ac.uk/) (bronze sponsor)

To register, complete the following form:
[https://forms.gle/NjXfManJWmwLX7sc7](https://forms.gle/NjXfManJWmwLX7sc7).

join remotely via Zoom.
