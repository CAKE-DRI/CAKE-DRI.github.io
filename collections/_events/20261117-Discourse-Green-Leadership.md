How can people working in digital research be supported to make it more environmentally sustainable? What is the role of digital research technical professionals in leading this transformation? How can we make environmental sustainability a valued and rewarded part of a career in digital research? Join us to discuss these questions and more!
- Tuesday 17 November
- Bayes Centre, University of Edinburgh
