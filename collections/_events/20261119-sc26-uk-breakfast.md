SC is enormous, and it is surprisingly easy to spend the whole week without crossing paths with colleagues from back home. So CAKE is hosting a breakfast for everyone from the UK at SC26.

Come along to meet other people from the UK research computing community, make new connections, catch up on how the conference is going for everyone and swap tips on what not to miss for the rest of the week. Best of all, breakfast is free!

**[Sign up for the UK Networking Breakfast](https://forms.gle/UszkMqNwqCux92aR6)**

## Who is it for?

The breakfast is for **SC26 attendees who are based in the UK**: researchers, RSEs, research technical professionals, system administrators, students, industry and anyone else working in or around UK HPC and research computing. It's an informal event and there is no agenda, just good food and good company.

Places are limited, so please sign up in advance using the form above. If you sign up and then find you can't make it, please let us know so we can offer your place to someone else.

## Where and when

**When:** Thursday 19 November 2026, 7:30am - 8:30am (CST)

**Where:** The Spoke & Bird (South Loop), 205 E 18th St, Chicago, IL 60616 ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Spoke+%26+Bird+205+E+18th+St+Chicago+IL+60616))

The Spoke & Bird is in Chicago's South Loop, a short walk from McCormick Place, so you'll have plenty of time to get to the convention centre for the start of Thursday's programme. **IMPORTANT NOTE: There are two Spoke & Birds in Chicago, this is the one in South Loop.**
