## Event information

<strong> *WP3 Professional Skills Training - Open Meeting on 23th of September, 3pm-4pm* </strong> 

At this Work Package 3 (Professional Skills Training) Open Meeting, we will discuss and, where appropriate, approve the new tasks proposed for the work package.

 If you would like to <strong>[suggest a new task for discussion](https://shareing-dri.github.io/about/suggest-a-task), please submit it by 22 September</strong>. This will give us time to collate the suggestions and prepare them for discussion during the meeting.

The aim is to make sure that WP3 reflects the needs of the wider dRTP community and supports the development of professional skills, learning pathways and opportunities for progression across different roles and levels of experience.
