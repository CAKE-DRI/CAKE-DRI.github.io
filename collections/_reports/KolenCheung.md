Kolen Cheung, a Research Software Engineer at the University of Exeter who supports researchers on GW4 Isambard 3, attended the HPC-AI Advisory Council 8th Annual UK Conference in Leicester on 13–14 October 2026. He caught up with Richard Gilham (Bristol Centre for Supercomputing) on how agents are used on Isambard-AI, and Gökmen Kilic (DiRAC Training Academy) on serving training as notebooks, and wrote up the UK’s new structure of national compute and the UK-LLM keynote.
