Dear colleagues, 

Since childhood, I have dreamed of visiting New Zealand and exploring distant countries and continents. This year, I had the opportunity to be part of the [40th International Conference on Advanced Information Networking and Applications (AINA 2026)](https://voyager.ce.fit.ac.jp/conf/aina/2026/), hosted by Victoria University of Wellington, New Zealand. The conference was held in a hybrid format, and I participated remotely via Zoom. I presented my paper, “Sleptsov Net Virtual Machine Implementation on GPU,” during the AINA-S26 session. The presentation was followed by a productive discussion, with particular interest in the proposed novel approach for efficiently selecting fireable transitions within a net extended with priority arcs. I would like to sincerely thank the CAKE team for supporting my research. I look forward to seeing you all at future conference venues. 

Best wishes, 

Tatiana R. Shmeleva.
