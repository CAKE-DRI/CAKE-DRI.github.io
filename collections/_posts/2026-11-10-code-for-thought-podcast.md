**CAKE features in the latest episode of the Code for Thought podcast, "Hmm... a special CAKE for you".**

In this episode, host Peter Schmidt talks to **Eleanor Broadway**, **Marion Weinzierl** and **Nick Brown** about what CAKE is, why it was set up and how it supports knowledge exchange and collaboration across the UK's Digital Research Infrastructure (DRI) communities. The conversation covers CAKE's work to map the [DRI landscape](/landscape/), the [funding opportunities](/funds/) we run and share with the community, and how people can get involved.

The episode also hears from **Tobias Weinzierl** and **Eva Fernandez-Amez**, who explain how the [Durham HPC Days](/events/202606-DurhamHPCDays/) connect with CAKE and bring the HPC and research computing community together.

**About Code for Thought:**

[Code for Thought](https://codeforthought.buzzsprout.com/) is a podcast about research software and the people who build and use it, covering everything from software engineering practice to the communities and infrastructure that support computational research.
