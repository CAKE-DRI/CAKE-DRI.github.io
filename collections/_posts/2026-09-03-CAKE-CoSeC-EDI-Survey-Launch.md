**Help us understand experiences, priorities and challenges around equality, diversity and inclusion (EDI) across the UK DRI+ community.**

The CAKE-CoSeC EDI Working Group is running a short survey to better understand what is working well, where gaps or challenges remain, and what information, resources and support would be most helpful to the community. 

Your responses will help shape the work of the CAKE-CoSeC EDI Working Group, ensuring that its priorities and activities reflect the experiences and needs of the community it supports.

**About the CAKE-CoSeC EDI Working Group:**

The CAKE-CoSeC EDI Working Group is a collaborative initiative between CAKE and [Computational Science Centre for Research Communities (CoSeC)](https://www.cosec.ac.uk), to promote and support a culture of EDI across the UK DRI landscape. The group aims to deliver tangible, community-driven actions and offering strategic advice on inclusive practices in areas such as communication, recruitment, research, training, and event organisation. 

*Learn more about the [CAKE-CoSeC EDI Working Group.](https://www.cake.ac.uk/CAKEbox/edia/CAKE-CoSeC-EDI-WG/)*

<br>

**Whether you have lots of experience with EDI or are just starting to think about these issues, we want to hear from you. Your voice can help shape what comes next, please take the survey and share your experience with us.**
