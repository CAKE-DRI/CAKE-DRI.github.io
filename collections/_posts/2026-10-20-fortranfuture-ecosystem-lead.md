**Mission: help (re)build a global scientific and engineering community powered by Fortran.**

[FortranFuture](https://www.jobs.ac.uk/job/DTD796/ecosystem-lead-senior-research-fellow-06-fte),
a new EPSRC-funded Network+ led by Queen's University Belfast with the
University of Southampton, is recruiting an **Ecosystem Lead** at Senior
Research Fellow level. This is a part-time (0.6 FTE) post based in the School of
Electronics, Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at Queen's.

## About the role

Fortran continues to underpin critical codes across science and engineering,
from weather and climate to nuclear and materials modelling. The Ecosystem Lead
will drive the work of building a global ecosystem around the language and its
community, including:

* Building a sustainable community and research network that will last beyond
  the funded period of the project
* Forecasting possible futures for the Fortran ecosystem and roadmapping the
  opportunities and challenges ahead
* Contributing to the strategic oversight and allocation of the network's
  flexible funding, and helping to secure future funding

The team is open-minded about who they recruit, whether that is someone more
technical, someone more community-oriented, or someone in between. They are
also open to remote working.

## Key details

* **Employer:** Queen's University Belfast
* **Hours:** 0.6 FTE
* **Contract:** Fixed term to August 2029, with the possibility of extension
  (depending on funding)
* **Application deadline:** 28 October 2026
